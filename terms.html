1. What Galla Petti does

Galla Petti is a digital payment book (ledger) for businesses. It records the payments your business receives by reading bank “payment received” SMS and payment-app notifications on your phone (with your permission), shows daily totals and history, exports Excel/PDF reports, announces payments by voice, sends morning and evening summaries, and lets you share your payment book with linked devices (staff or family).

SMS and notification access are used only to identify incoming payment-received alerts from banks and payment apps and record them in your payment book. Galla Petti does not read, store or upload personal messages, conversations or OTPs.

3. Accuracy – please always verify

Entries depend on SMS and notifications delivered to your phone by banks, telecom operators and payment apps, which we do not control. Messages can be delayed, missed, changed in format, or blocked by phone battery settings. An entry or voice alert is a convenience record, not proof that money has reached your bank. Always confirm important or high-value payments in your bank or payment app before handing over goods or services. Entries marked “Pending sync”, “Suspected duplicate” or similar are advisory flags for you to check.

Voice alerts and pop-ups depend on your phone’s volume, its Text-to-Speech engine, Bluetooth/speaker connection, notification settings and battery-optimisation settings (which some phone brands apply strongly). A missing, late or repeated voice alert or pop-up is not, by itself, a failure of the service.
