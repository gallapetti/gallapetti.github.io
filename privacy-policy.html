Privacy Policy

App: Galla Petti – UPI Payment Book (package com.gallapetti.app)
Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Effective: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Galla Petti app (“Galla Petti”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, how it is used and protected, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The App is operated by Vadde Kavya, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. It applies to everyone who uses the App (“you”, “merchant”).

In short: Galla Petti reads only payment messages and payment notifications on your phone to build your business payment book. Personal chats, OTPs and promotional messages are filtered out on your phone and are never uploaded. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for profiling.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

  • Mobile number – to create and verify your account (by OTP) and to identify your payment book.
  • Account / shop nickname and your preferred voice language.
  • Linked device and shared-account details – mobile numbers of staff or family members you choose to link.
  • Bank SMS format samples – only if you choose to type or paste one in “Report bank format”.
  • Support emails you send us.

b) Payment information read from your phone (with your permission)

Bank SMS (SMS permission). With your permission (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS), the App checks incoming SMS on your phone and keeps only credit / payment-received messages sent by banks. All other SMS – personal messages, OTPs, promotions, debit and EMI alerts – are ignored on your phone and are not uploaded.

Payment app notifications (Notification access). With your permission, the App reads notifications from payment and banking apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, bank apps and their business versions) and from your SMS/messaging app, and keeps only “payment received” notifications. Notifications from chat, social media and other apps are ignored on your phone.

From each accepted payment message or notification we store:

  • amount, date and time; transaction reference (UTR / RRN / UPI reference) when present;
  • payer name as shown in the message; bank name; masked account ending (e.g. XX1234); the payment app or sender;
  • the text of that payment message/notification, kept with the entry so it can be checked and reconciled (e.g. to avoid duplicates and resolve “pending” entries).

We never ask for, read or store bank passwords, UPI PIN, card numbers, CVV or OTPs.

c) Device and app information

  • An app-generated device ID (to manage linked devices) and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token (to deliver payment voice alerts to your linked devices).
  • SIM slot selection (phone state permission) – used on your phone to match a bank SMS to the correct account when you use two SIMs.
  • Crash and problem reports – kept on your phone only; shared with us only if you send them.

d) Subscription and referral information

  • Your plan, start/expiry dates and, for paid plans, the Google Play purchase reference used to verify the purchase. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details – payments are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Your referral code and referral status.

2. How we use information

  • To record your received payments in your payment book and show daily totals, history and reports (Excel/PDF exports are created on your phone).
  • To announce payments by voice on your phone and on your linked devices, and to send the 8:30 AM / 8:30 PM summaries.
  • To detect duplicates and match app notifications with bank SMS.
  • To verify your mobile number, manage linked devices, subscriptions, the free trial and referral rewards.
  • To improve bank-message recognition (using format samples you submit).
  • To provide support, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit scoring or lending, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

4. Storage, security and retention

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured cloud servers with access controls.
  • No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will inform you as required by law if a breach affects you.
  • We keep your data while your account is active. After a verified deletion request we delete it within 30 days (see Delete Account), except purchase records that tax/accounting law requires us to keep (up to 8 years).

5. Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: you can turn off SMS, notification access and other permissions anytime in Android settings. Automatic recording and voice alerts will then stop.
  • Access and correction: your payment book is visible in the App; email us to correct account details.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete Account in the App, or email us – see Delete Account.
  • Withdraw consent / grievances: under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Act, 2000, you may withdraw consent, request correction or erasure, and raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (below). You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.
