Delete Your Galla Petti Account

App: Galla Petti – UPI Payment Book (Google Play) · Developer contact: gallapetti.galla@gmail.com

Option 1 – Inside the app

  1. Open Galla Petti.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. The account is removed from your phone and all devices linked to it are disconnected.

To also erase your data from our servers, send the request below (Option 2). You can use Option 2 even if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 2 – Request by email (app not required)

Send an email from any address to gallapetti.galla@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Galla Petti account deletion request
  • Your registered mobile number

Email deletion request

To protect your data, we may send a one-time verification code (OTP) to the registered mobile number before deleting. We complete verified requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and subscription records (plan, dates, Google Play order reference) may be kept for up to 8 years where required for tax and accounting law, then deleted.
  • Information needed to resolve an open dispute, prevent fraud or comply with a legal obligation is kept only as long as that need lasts.
  • Encrypted server backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle, within 90 days.

Payment records held by your bank or by payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) are not stored by us and are not affected.
