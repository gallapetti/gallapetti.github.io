Delete Your Galla Petti Account

App: Galla Petti – UPI Payment Book (Google Play) · Developer: Budde Bharath Kumar · Contact: gallapetti.galla@gmail.com

Option 1 – Inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Galla Petti.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Type the last 4 digits of the mobile number shown, then tap Yes, Delete.

For an account you own, this removes the account from your phone, disconnects every linked device, and deletes its data from our servers immediately. No email is needed. Only the owner’s phone can delete an account; a phone with shared access cannot. Deletion cannot be undone. If you later add the same number again, it starts as a new account (without a new free trial); payments can be read again only from bank SMS still on your phone.

For a shared account (someone else’s payment book you were given access to), only your phone’s access is removed; the owner’s data stays with the owner.

Option 2 – By email (if the app is uninstalled or the phone is lost)

Email gallapetti.galla@gmail.com with the subject Galla Petti account deletion request and your registered mobile number.

Email deletion request

To protect your data we may first send a one-time code (OTP) to the registered number. Verified requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by email.

Option 3 – Delete only your transaction history (keep your account)

To keep your account and plan but erase your recorded payment history from our servers, email us with the subject Galla Petti transaction data deletion request and your registered mobile number. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

Email data-only request

What is kept, and for how long

  • Paid Google Play purchase records (plan, dates, Google Play order reference) are kept for up to 8 years for tax and accounting, with your mobile number removed, and then deleted.
  • A one-way coded value of your number that only records that the free trial was already used (fraud prevention). It cannot be turned back into your number.
  • Data needed for an open dispute or a legal obligation is kept only as long as that need lasts.
  • Deleted data is removed from our live (production) database immediately when the request is processed; copies in encrypted disaster-recovery backups are purged on a rolling cycle within 90 days.

Inactive accounts

An account with no activity for 180 days (no payment recorded and the App not opened on any linked phone) is deleted in the same way. We notify you in the App at 150 days and every day from 175 days, and never delete earlier than 30 days after the first notice. Opening the App once keeps the account.

Payment records held by your bank or by payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) are not stored by us and are not affected.
